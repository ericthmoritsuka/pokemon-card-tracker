## New findings

- **New:** if the page is hidden before the scanner camera finishes starting, the camera keeps running while hidden. The stream arrives after `stopCamera()` ran with `camera` still null, and only `alive` is checked (`js/scan/view.js:963-984`, `:1103-1109`). In `a/e03-scan.mjs` case b, the track was still `live` 4 s after hiding.
- **New:** the landscape spread does not fit above the tab bar even without the disc. `--bs-chrome` (`css/binder-spread.css:763`) subtracts only the tab bar and 24 px, and leaves out about 30 px for the "Page N of M" line and the zip. This explains the roadmap item better than the disc's 28 px rise.
- **New:** the update toast offers Reload only when `hadController` was true at page load (`app.js:247`). A page opened on the very first visit later gets "Card Tracker now opens offline." with no Reload, even for a real update. Cosmetic.
- **New (part of E-07):** `permanent()` treats 401 as permanent (`js/photos/store.js:453-457`), so an expired-token refusal skips the 30 s retry timer until the next flush trigger.
- **New (part of E-26):** finish records are cached only in the screen's own `Map` (`js/pokemon-cards-view.js:352`). A warm reopen was no faster than a cold one at 1x.
- **New (part of E-27):** `handTicks()` rebuilds a `Set` from `Object.keys` on every call (`js/checklists.js:67-69`), and a draw calls it 2,050 times, so every draw of a list slows as its hand ticks grow.
- **New (read in code only):** after Back with Add photo open, Capture and Save still work against the previous card's copy, and `onSaved` runs on an already destroyed carousel (`js/photos/index.js:100-112`).
- **Correction to E-05 scope:** null-language copies are dropped along with de, es, and it, so the loss is wider than the audit states.
